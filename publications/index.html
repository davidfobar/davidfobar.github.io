2025

  1. CWMD
    The Imperative of Nuclear Literacy in Military Education: Insights from the NSERC Nuclear Deterrence Staff Ride
    David Fobar and T. Halbert
    Countering-WMD, 2025
    Fall/Winter 2025 issue, article begins on p. 47. Full issue hosted here; not peer-reviewed.
    Abs HTML PDF

2024

  1. CWMD
    Reconstructing Fallout Distributions: Using Convolutional Neural Networks to Reconstruct Nuclear Fallout Distributions from SCatterable RAdiation Monitors (SCRAM)
    Kevin T. Filip, David Fobar, T. Halbert, and 1 more author
    Countering-WMD, 2024
    Summer/Fall 2024 issue, article begins on p. 71. Full issue hosted here; not peer-reviewed.
    Abs HTML PDF

    Discusses various data sources available for generating artificial radiation fields in support of training a neural network that can reconstruct a continuous radiation intensity map from randomly scattered point detectors.
