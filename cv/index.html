Publications

  • 2025
    Development of a Pulsed UV-LED Calibration Technique for Scintillating Detectors
    Journal of Radiation Effects Research and Engineering, Vol. 44

    Introduces a method of conducting source-less energy calibration of radiation detectors using a small pulsed LED.

  • 2022
    A Machine Learning Approach to a Multidetector Array Response Function for Nuclear Search
    IEEE Transactions on Nuclear Science

    Investigated the novel use of machine learning to project a unit vector from an array of radiation detectors back to a point source.

  • 2021
    Considerations for Training an Artificial Neural Network for Particle Type Identification
    IEEE Transactions on Nuclear Science

    Used machine learning to conduct pulse shape discrimination between neutron and gamma-ray radiation particles interacting in an organic scintillating radiation detector.

  • 2018
    Robotic delivery of laser-induced breakdown spectroscopy for sensitive chlorine measurement in dry cask storage systems
    Progress in Nuclear Energy

    Developed a method of remotely characterizing chlorine deposition on the surface of stainless steel using dual-pulse LIBS for the in-situ inspection of dry-cask storage canisters of spent nuclear fuel.

  • 2018
    Measurement of chlorine concentration on steel surfaces via fiber-optic laser-induced breakdown spectroscopy in double-pulse configuration
    Spectrochimica Acta Part B, Atomic Spectroscopy

    Developed a method of characterizing chlorine deposition on the surface of stainless steel using dual-pulse laser induced breakdown spectroscopy.

Conference Presentations & Publications

  • Reconstructing Nuclear Fallout Fields with Scatterable Dosimeters — K. Filip, D. Fobar, S. Ague. 2024 IEEE Long Island Systems, Applications and Technology Conference. Documented the wireless infrastructure and data processing necessary to localize sensors using differential time-of-arrival, filtered with a Kalman filter.
  • Gamma ray directionality from a tightly packed scintillator array — D. Fobar, W. Koch, T. Bruff, P. Chapman. Presentation at SORMA XIX, May 2023. Supplements the RADBOT capstone and extends the Aug 2022 published work, using a physics-informed loss function with probabilistic weighting of source location.
  • Demonstration of a Low Power Imaging System for a Portable Time Projection Chamber — W. Koch, D. Fobar, T. Bruff, P. Chapman. Presentation at HEART, April 2023; pending publication in JRERE. Aims to provide real-time localization of fast neutron sources, a key indicator for special nuclear material.
  • Unsupervised Machine Learning Approaches to Nuclear Particle Type Classification — N. Liebers, J. Huckelberry, D. Ruiz, D. Fobar, P. Chapman. IEEE LISAT Conference Proceedings, May 2022. An unsupervised ML algorithm developed by cadets during a joint EECS/PANE independent research project.
